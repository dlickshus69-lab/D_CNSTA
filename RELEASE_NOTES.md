## What's new

- **The falling Nitro blows up where it lands**, also when Crash gets out of the way, so stay clear of the spot. The Nitro that appears ahead of Crash doesn't; it waits as before.
- **The Nitro that appears ahead of Crash is a little closer:** about 1.3 crates away instead of 1.5.
- **It can appear in midair.** If Crash is in the middle of a jump (or a fall), the Nitro appears straight ahead at his height and floats there. If the game lets it drop, the client holds it up (the log says `held up`).
- **Fewer Nitros inside walls.** A Nitro straight ahead now only goes where it's on the way to a crate further ahead, or next to floor Crash already stood on. Crates are always somewhere he can reach, so it no longer ends up in a wall where his path bends. If there's no safe spot ahead, the Nitro falls onto him instead, as before.
