## What's new

- **White is softer, so it shouldn't glow under strong lights.** In V1.2.44, white still glowed in very bright spots like the Crash 3 hub's bridge, because white fur reflects about five times as much light as Crash's orange. White now reflects about 40% less, close to Crash's own chest. Light gray and gray step down a little to stay distinct from it.
- **New color `bright_white`:** the brighter white from V1.2.44, for anyone who prefers it. It can glow under strong lights.
- If you're wearing white, the client makes your colored Crash again the next time you connect. Restart the game once afterward.
