## Testing build

This is the **Testing build**: it has what's still being worked on (Harder bosses). For playing a seed, the **Stable build** is the other release on this page. Its apworld is `crash_nst_testing.apworld`, the game **Crash Bandicoot N. Sane Trilogy (Testing)** (install it next to the Stable `crash_nst.apworld`; a YAML picks one with its `game:` line). This client plays Testing seeds and regular ones.

## What's new

- **No more white screen after a game over (DeathLink).** DeathLinks that came while you were at the game over screen were kept, and all struck as the level opened again, which left the screen white until you restarted the level. Now the ones that come at the game over screen are dropped (Crash is already out), and a DeathLink only strikes once the level is open: Crash able to move for 1.5 seconds with the level's HUD on screen.
- **Boxes count again after a level loads.** The client could keep listening to the crate script of the place it started listening in (the Crash 2 warp room before a level, or a level before a Continue after a game over), so no broken box counted any more, and nothing said so (MondoF in N. Sanity Beach, Eric after the warp room). Now it listens afresh in every new level and scene, and whenever a level's crate script is set up again.
- **When a DeathLink or a trap waits, the log says why.** A DeathLink still waiting after 10 seconds (`DeathLink: waiting ...`), or traps standing still for 15 seconds while Crash plays (`Traps wait: ...`), now names what the client sees as the reason. Guicho's DeathLinks didn't strike and his traps stood still with nothing in his log; if it happens again, his log will show why.
- **DeathLink in Hang Eight, Air Crash and Plant Food:** those levels mix jet-board parts with parts on foot, and the whole level counted as a ride, so a DeathLink waited ("on a ride") even while walking. Now it strikes on foot as anywhere else, and waits only while Crash is on the board.
- **DeathLink on rides (research):** in a gimmick level (hog, polar bear, jet ski, jetpack, plane, bike, jet board...), the log now lists the messages the ride's scripts get (`Ride research:` lines). Play one and crash the ride once, then send the log: the message that ends a ride the game's own way is what a DeathLink there can send, so riders lose a life right away instead of when they're next on foot.
- **Crash colors fixed when the saved Crash.pak already had colors** (after `CrashNSTAP\skins` was lost while Crash wore an older seed's colors, the new colors only partly took). The client now checks the copy it colors from: if it already has colors in it, it says so and asks you to verify the game's files in Steam. After a verify, the colors are made again at the next connection (so everyone's colored Crash is made once more with this update: restart the game when it says so).
- **`/colors random`** (asked for): random colors for this seed, kept until `/colors seed` (back to your YAML's) or a new seed. They show from the next game start.
- **Box logic fixes from the Stable V1.3.1 run** (new seeds only, Ability Shuffle): Piston It Away needs Slide; High Time's bonus round needs Crouch Jump (a stack of four iron crates is in the way); Jungle Rollers needs Spin for its crate wall after the first checkpoint, so for the clear, the boxes past the wall and the bonus round; every crate in a stack three high needs Spin (was four); a crate right under an iron-bound crate needs Spin (The Pits' bonus round). A full pass over every level's boxes is coming next.
