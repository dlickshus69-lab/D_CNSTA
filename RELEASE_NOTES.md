## Testing build

This is the **Testing build**: it has what's still being worked on (Harder bosses). For playing a seed, the **Stable build** is the other release on this page. Its apworld is `crash_nst_testing.apworld`, the game **Crash Bandicoot N. Sane Trilogy (Testing)** (install it next to the Stable `crash_nst.apworld`; a YAML picks one with its `game:` line). This client plays Testing seeds and regular ones.

## What's new

- **Papu Papu: his spin from where he slammed, another try** (Eric's V1.3.49 run: the record the game copies his place and turn from wasn't picked up: the search near his physics found none, and the watch caught the game's physics step but looked the record up afterwards, when the game's list had already moved on). This build takes that record from the register the step itself loads it into, so the watch finds it in the first few frames of his stand after your first aimed slam (`his physics' source, from the physics step the watch caught`), and from then on the aim turns that record too. The search near his physics also looks a little wider. If the game puts that record back too and his spin still jumps, a second watch goes on it, to see what writes it. Test: the test seed with `/boss papu`, a few of his slams, then the log and a clip.
