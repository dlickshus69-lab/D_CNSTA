## What's new

- **Crash colors no longer glow.** White fur glowed under bright lights because many of its highlights were pushed to pure white. Light colors like yellow and light blue did the same. Highlights now ease off before full white, so no color is brighter than normal Crash's own fur at its brightest. White is a little softer too.
- **Your colored Crash updates itself.** A colored Crash made by an older client is made again the next time you connect. Restart the game once afterward to see it.
