## What's new

- **Nitro Trap in more gimmick levels.** It now also strikes on the hog (Hog Wild, Whole Hog), the Crash 2 jet board (Hang Eight, Air Crash, Plant Food) and polar bear (Bear It, Bear Down, Un-Bearable, Totally Bear), and Coco's jet ski (Makin' Waves, Tell No Tales, Ski Crazed, Hot Coco). The chase levels already had it. It stays off in the jetpack, motorcycle, plane, scuba, Coco's tiger and Baby T levels. It still needs a level with Nitro crates, so Crash 1 levels don't get it yet.
- **Nitro research:** `/nitro_probe` now also writes to the log how the game makes a Nitro, and which Nitro files the game's archives and memory hold. This is the first step toward a Nitro Trap in any level.
- **Crash colors:** the thin pinkish-orange edge by Crash's mouth now takes your chest color. The lips stay a dark mouth line. If you wear colors, the client makes your colored Crash again the next time you connect. Restart the game once afterward.
