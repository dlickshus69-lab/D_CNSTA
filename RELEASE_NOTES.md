## What's new

- **Crash colors: no more orange at the corner of Crash's grin.** His lips, the band along his mouth and its pink corners, now take a dark shade of your chest color. Before, they kept the game's red, which looked orange in the game's light. The inside of his mouth stays red.
- If you wear colors, the client makes your colored Crash again the next time you connect. Restart the game once afterward. No new seed needed.
