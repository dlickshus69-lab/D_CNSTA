## Testing build

This is the **Testing build**: it has what's still being worked on (Harder bosses). For playing a seed, the **Stable build** is the other release on this page. Its apworld is `crash_nst_testing.apworld`, the game **Crash Bandicoot N. Sane Trilogy (Testing)** (install it next to the Stable `crash_nst.apworld`; a YAML picks one with its `game:` line). This client plays Testing seeds and regular ones.

Now the client turns the flying rock itself around: it measures the rock's speed, finds where that's kept, and points it back at Crash. This build is for finding the right spot, so if it works, the rock turns back early in its flight, not right at him. Getting the timing right at him comes next.

- His 8-notch health bar is unchanged.
